01

Tokyo

Tokyo was mesmerizing from the moment we stepped out. Late night konbini runs, figuring out trains somehow, vegan ramen in Shibuya, fruit sandos almost every day, freezing walks saved by hand warmers, Asakusa at night, fortunes, endless city lights and even a birthday in between all the chaos. Somehow, most of it went as planned. The rest made it better. Tokyo was exciting, chaotic, beautiful and completely worth the hype.

Story moment

Core Memory

Shibuya Sky was probably one of the most awaited moments of the trip for us, and the views completely lived up to it. From the top, every direction felt like a different frame. In one, you could see people flowing through the famous Shibuya Crossing. In another, the busy streets and traffic looked almost tiny from above. And then there was our favourite view, the endless city skyline with Tokyo Tower standing in between all those tall buildings. We just stood there for a good 10 to 15 minutes, doing nothing but looking at the city. It was freezing that night, but honestly, nobody cared. The view was too good to leave. And of course, even in that cold, we still forced our friends to stand around for portraits. Priorities.

02

Kyoto

Kyoto was a short trip, but completely worth it. The contrast from Tokyo was incredible. Everything felt calmer, slower and much more rooted in culture. The torii gates looked similar at first, but somehow we kept finding interesting frames everywhere. We explored temples and shrines, saw people walking around in kimono and yukata, and even worked up the courage to photograph a few strangers. The best part came at the end. Sakura season had not really started yet, so we searched for a spot far from the usual places. When we finally reached it, the entire garden was in bloom. Worth every bit of the journey.

Story moment

Early Bloom

We were in Japan during the second week of March, a little too early for sakura season. After finding a few posts about an early bloom, we decided to take a chance and travel all the way there. It paid off. The entire garden was in bloom. We also saw so many people beautifully dressed in kimono and finally built up the courage to ask a few strangers if we could take their portraits. They were surprisingly chill about it, and for something completely new to us, we came away with some of our favourite shots. Glad we took the chance on that little Instagram find. We got the sakura experience we were hoping for and some photographs worth remembering.
