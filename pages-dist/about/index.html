Behind the camera

About

I'm Prathik, a photographer collecting small observations from the road.

This site is a home for the moments that sit between destinations: changing light, passing gestures, and everyday places with a story of their own.

Approach · 01

Slow looking. Honest frames.

One camera, no fixed shot list, and usually no idea what I’ll come back with.

I photograph places the way I notice them. Through light, colour, people, and the small details that give them character.

Nothing too perfect. Just frames that feel like they belonged there.
